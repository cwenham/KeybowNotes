Installing KeybowNotes
======================

1. Drag KeybowNotes onto the Applications folder beside it.
2. Open it from Applications. A keyboard icon appears in the menu bar.

KeybowNotes needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.


If macOS won't open it
----------------------

This copy of KeybowNotes isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open
it macOS says it can't check it, and offers only Done or Move to Trash.
Choose Done, then:

1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
2. Scroll down to Security, where it says KeybowNotes was blocked.
3. Click Open Anyway, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

macOS remembers, and opens it straight away from then on. Each new version
you install asks once again.

Or, in Terminal, after dragging it to Applications:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/KeybowNotes.app


What it will ask for
--------------------

KeybowNotes asks for each permission the first time something needs it — and
those prompts can open behind other windows:

- Removable volumes, to set a keypad up.
- Notes, Mail, Music: to control them, for the actions that use them.
- Calendars and Reminders, Contacts, Location: for the actions that use them.
- Accessibility, to type text and read the selection in other apps.
- Local Network, to talk to Home Assistant.
- Media & Apple Music, to list your music when you set up music keys.
